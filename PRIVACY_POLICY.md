# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Phone Number & Profile Information
To use the App, you must register using your phone number and a display name. We use this to create your account and identify you to your friends.

### B. Contact List (Address Book)
With your explicit permission, the App accesses your device's contact list. 
**Why?** We upload the phone numbers from your contact list securely to our servers solely to find out which of your friends are already using the App. 
**How we store it:** We do not permanently store your contact list on our servers. The numbers are only used momentarily to perform a matching query against our existing users.

### C. Location Data (GPS)
The core feature of the App is sharing your real-time location with chosen friends. With your explicit permission, we collect your precise location data (GPS).
**Why?** To allow you to share your location with a specific friend for a limited time, or to respond to a location request.
**How we store it:** Location data is highly sensitive. When you share your location, it is stored on our servers for **maximum 10 minutes**. After 10 minutes, the location data automatically expires and is no longer accessible by anyone.

### D. Device and Push Notification Data
We collect your device's push notification token (e.g., Apple Push Notification token or Expo Push token) to send you alerts when someone requests your location or shares their location with you.

## 2. How We Share Your Information
We **never** sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties. Your data is only shared in the following ways:
*   **With your friends:** Your display name and location (when actively shared by you) are visible only to the specific friend you choose to share it with, and only for 10 minutes.
*   **Service Providers:** We may use third-party services (such as cloud hosting or push notification providers like Expo) to operate the App. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and only process data on our behalf.
*   **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, we may disclose your information to law enforcement or governmental agencies.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
*   **Location Data:** Automatically expires and becomes inaccessible after 10 minutes.
*   **Account Data:** We retain your phone number, display name, and push token for as long as your account is active.
*   **Deletion:** You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

## 4. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, if the changes are significant, through an in-app notification.
